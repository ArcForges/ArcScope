# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss the work, avoid personal attacks, and respect different experience levels and backgrounds. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation. Report conduct issues privately to a repository maintainer through their available GitHub contact. Do not post another person's private information in a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports confidentially and explain moderation decisions when doing so will not expose private information.
